Storage retention

When enabled, cleanup runs every minute and removes oldest events to maintain the reserve. Active recordings and events less than two minutes old are protected. Training data is never removed.

Telegram alerts

Camera health

One alert per outage, followed by a recovery message after five seconds of fresh frames. Health alerts also appear in Event history and ignore alert schedules.

Reduce repeated alerts

Uses the alert cooldown in Detection. Set confirmation to 0 for immediate alerts. Fall detection and inspection voting retain their own confirmation rules.

Versioned datasets

Each build is a snapshot. At least 2 training and 2 validation images are required; use a larger, varied dataset for meaningful results. Identical images across splits are rejected.

Train a custom detector

Training runs in a separate process and may reduce live detection speed. It creates a candidate model; activation is a separate step.

Model library

Compare models on the same validation dataset. Precision measures false alarms; recall measures missed objects. Higher scores are better, but small datasets can be misleading.
